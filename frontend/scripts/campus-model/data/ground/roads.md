# Road sections and junctions

`fit/roadSections.mjs` proposes records. It does not publish markings and never
overwrites `road-sections.json` or `junctions.json`. Todo 23 owns the generator;
todo 24 owns campus-wide curation. Photo pixels and chip sheets stay outside Git.

For this increment, run tests on the Mac through the heavy-command wrapper;
the lab is reserved for model builds.
From `frontend/`, run the estimator through the heavy-command wrapper:

```sh
/Users/gahn/.cache/scnu-heavy/scnu-heavy bun scripts/campus-model/fit/roadSections.mjs \
  --segment RD_093 \
  --out /private/tmp/road-sections.proposals.json \
  --chips /private/tmp/road-section-chips
bun scripts/campus-model/lib/schemas.mjs roadSections /private/tmp/road-sections.proposals.json
```

The input is `backend/simulation/data/road_segments.geojson`, using each
feature's `properties.epsg5186` paths. `pathIndex` separates branches of a
MultiLineString. Distances start at each path's first vertex. Positive lateral
offset is left of the directed path. Sections are ordered, non-overlapping
`fromM`/`toM` intervals. Optional `splitAtM` lists internal boundaries on path 0.
Widths, kerb offsets, lane counts and other unreviewed values remain estimates.

`validate("roadSections", document)` accepts a segment-keyed object:

```json
{
  "RD_093": {
    "sections": [{
      "pathIndex": 0,
      "fromM": 0,
      "toM": 20,
      "lanes": [{"dir": "fwd", "widthM": 3.25}, {"dir": "bwd", "widthM": 3.25}],
      "centre": {"type": "yellow_solid", "widthM": 0.15},
      "edges": {"left": "none", "right": "none"},
      "lateralOffsetM": 0,
      "kerbOffsetsM": {"left": 3.25, "right": 3.25},
      "kerbNoParking": {"left": false, "right": false},
      "humps": [],
      "evidence": {"tier": "T4", "refs": ["illustrative example, not surveyed"], "photoSupport": null},
      "estimated": true,
      "reason": "Illustrative schema record only; not campus curation."
    }]
  }
}
```

Centre types are `none`, `yellow_solid`, `yellow_double`, `yellow_dashed`.
Edge types are `none`, `white_solid`, `white_dashed`, `yellow_solid`,
`yellow_dashed`. A hump has `atM`, `lengthM`, `heightM`, and `bands`.
`dashPhaseM`, if supplied, is nonnegative.

The estimator samples profiles every 0.5 m, excludes the building/crown/shadow
mask, and requires 60% visible-length yellow support. Two separated peaks in
the one-metre transverse profile indicate a double line. Lesser support does
not prove absence: a section may need splitting, a better centreline, or VR
review. All-hidden input is T4 with null support, never a zero measurement.
Arrow and text detections become `markingCandidates` with along-path and
signed lateral coordinates. Their direction or text is not guessed from blobs.
Chip sheets show the photo beside the masked photo and the proposed reference
line in cyan, including when no painted centre line is proposed.

`validate("junctions", document)` accepts a network-node-keyed object. Each
node has `approaches`; each approach has:

- `segment`, `pathIndex` (default 0), and `end: "from" | "to"`.
- `stopLineSetbackM` (null or distance from that endpoint).
- `crossing` (null or `{widthM, offsetM, zebraPeriodM, barWidthM}`).
- `arrows: [{type, lane, atM}]` and `text: [{text, lane, atM}]`.
  Arrow types: `straight`, `left`, `right`, `straight_left`, `straight_right`,
  `uturn`. Lane indices are zero-based.
- `humps`, `signalArms: [{bearingDeg, lengthM}]`,
  `yieldTriangles: [{points}]`, and `guideLines: [{points}]`.
  Points are EPSG:5186; angles are clockwise from north.
- `evidence`, `estimated`, and a `reason` for T4.

`road-parcels.json` contains the reviewed E3 island carriageway and the missing
road under three existing zebra bars near TM (244084,263874).
`road-ground.json`, generated by `fit/roadGround.mjs`, replaces the remaining
support hulls with photo-boundary sections, dimensioned road priors, existing
road/lot references, or curved junction-kerb contours. Old ids remain only for
consumer compatibility. `road-support-evidence.json` is the immutable migration
inventory from fa98b455; its old rings locate chips but are never rendered.

Each record has a road id, section dimensions, photo visibility/asphalt shares,
profile counts, method, evidence, and a numerical reason. Where the road source
has no width, the estimator records the median of its finite campus widths,
not null or a surveyed value. Every generated record remains `estimated:true`.
The road id is the nearest network candidate, not a verified connection;
`roadDistanceM` exposes its separation. A distant candidate does not establish
that the local paved patch belongs to that segment. The local `section` and
surface proposal remain separate from the candidate's `roadSection` proposal.
The applicable fallback is the binding design document, sections 2 and 21:

> T4 추정: 위 어느 것도 없을 때. 반드시 `estimated: true`와 이유.
> 승인 없는 제안은 `estimated: true`로 빌드에 들어간다.

This permits dimensioned, reasoned proposals, not an unowned old hull or an
accuracy pass. A hidden pixel is never evidence that pavement does not exist.
Road surfaces replacing estimated aprons are clipped against surveyed
non-road polygons; a proposal cannot silently overpaint an A003 sidewalk.

`parcelsSeed.mjs` no longer calls the support-hull generator. It consumes the
new records, checks the migration inventory, and writes `roadRecords` plus
`paintDecisions` with the final map. An excluded paint item must name a record,
the conflicting parcel/class and the outside/total sample count. Section 5's
paint-on-road/lot constraint stays enforced. A curved marking whose vertex mean
falls outside its own band is checked at every triangle interior, not at the
false point inside the island.

Regenerate in order, through the heavy wrapper for photo/model work:

```sh
bun scripts/campus-model/fit/roadGround.mjs --chips /private/tmp/road-ground-chips
bun scripts/campus-model/fit/parcelsSeed.mjs --write
bun scripts/campus-model/fit/siteDetailAnchors.mjs --write
bun scripts/campus-model/fit/objectAnchorsSeed.mjs --write
```

Then run the plan's model/AO rebuild chain. The anchor seeds retain original
world positions while recomputing their coordinates in the changed parcels.

## Record generator and migration status

`stages/roadMarkings.mjs` exports `roadMarkingPlan({sections, junctions, context})`.
It returns scene-coordinate `pieces` for `buildMarkings`, per-record `humps`
for `humpLayers`/`buildHumpMeshes`, and a metre-valued `heightAt(x,z)` with
`heightAt.near` for terrain refinement and height-sidecar sampling.
`humpProfile` is shared with the seed-only evidence reader. Ground hump
layers and walls use each record's length and height when supplied.

The generator validates both documents before producing geometry. It reads no
photo detections or gate. Section stations belong to the directed reference
path, including on bends; dashes use phase zero at `fromM`, or the supplied
`dashPhaseM` as the elapsed distance in their cycle. Yellow dashes use 3/3 m,
white dashes 3/5 m. Double lines have a 0.15 m clear gap. These inherited shape
defaults are not new measurements.

Lane indices run right to left along the path, with forward lanes followed by
backward lanes. Opposing directions meet at the reference centre; one-way
lanes are centred on it. An optional `line: "white_solid" | "white_dashed"`
on a lane records its separator from the next same-direction lane. Omitted
separators produce no paint. Kerb offsets are measured from the shifted
reference, not inferred from detected fragments.

An approach's `from` end receives backward traffic; `to` receives forward
traffic. Stop lines span incoming lanes only. Crossing `offsetM` and hump
`atM` measure inward from that endpoint. Glyph `atM` measures upstream from
the stop line (or endpoint when no stop line is recorded); `lane` uses the
section's index. Missing sections, outgoing glyph lanes and unsupported text
raise record-named errors. Arrow/text template sizes are estimated.

Explicit export, from `frontend/`:

```sh
bun scripts/campus-model/stages/roadMarkings.mjs \
  --sections /private/tmp/road-sections.json \
  --junctions /private/tmp/junctions.json \
  --out /private/tmp/road-markings.json
```

The campus build now calls `loadRoadMarkings` and reads the published
`road-sections.json` and `junctions.json`. It never calls the evidence loader.
`fit/roadMarkingsSeed.mjs --write` regenerates those documents;
`--check` recomputes them and rejects stale bytes. Both commands use the heavy
wrapper. `fit/markingEvidence.mjs` and `fit/markingEvidenceFit.mjs` perform
the evidence support/fit step; `fit/humpEvidence.mjs` reads hump evidence.
These modules are seed/QA inputs only. `network/stalls.mjs` is removed.
All five photographic marking files live under `data/photo/evidence/`.

The seed fits semantic axes and grouped crossing grids into
`road.alignments[id].line` (EPSG:5186), referenced by `section.alignment`
and `approach.alignment`. `approach.section` identifies its actual section.
Longitudinal geometry uses `centre.type`, lane separator types, edge types,
kerb offsets and widths. `dashPattern: {dashM,gapM}` and `dashPhaseM` control
repeated dashes; there is no list of saved dash rectangles.

Crossing bar counts use the section's total lane width. `omittedBars` records
unobserved slots, not proven absence. Stops, arrows, text, symbols and areas
use typed junction controls. Arrows/text accept optional `lengthM` and `widthM`;
symbols and areas carry a known template, dimensions, lane and station.
No arbitrary polygon/region or `details` renderer remains. Nonempty legacy
`details` are rejected. Actual published-data mutation tests require width,
kerb offset, line type and crossing offset changes to change production paint.

Hump paint uses the lane cross-section. Optional `hump.widthM` and
`hump.lateralOffsetM` describe the raised body separately when it extends
beyond the paintable carriageway. These preserve the original height field.
`bandAxis: "travel"`, `bandWidthM` and `bandStartColour` control its stripe
pattern. The seed derives the paintable cross-section from the parcel map.

Stable ids are `RA_<hash>`, `RS_<hash>` and `JA_<hash>`, derived from semantic
role, sorted source refs and the component within that source. Geometry,
global ordering and RD/ND containers are not identity inputs. Curators retain
these ids when changing widths, positions or road ownership. Each emitted
piece has `objectId`, `partId`, `ownerId` and the compatible `record`.
All crossing bars share `JA_<hash>/crossing`; a bar's record adds its grid
index and its part id adds `/part/0`. Other object ids name the section or
approach control, with `/part/<localIndex>` for individual polygons.
`piece.id` remains a source-key compatibility field, not a logical object id.

Every inferred alignment records `association.verified:false` and candidate
road distance. Nearest RD/ND does not establish road ownership, traffic
connectivity or accuracy. Todo 24 must curate those associations; unverified
ownership blocks its acceptance even when geometry-control tests pass.

The build retains the explicit invalid parking divider exclusion and clips
all paint against that excluded pedestrian parcel, including neighbouring
stripes whose centres are outside it. The actual GLB, rather than a piece
centre alone, is checked for overlap. Existing parcel and height-sidecar
tests remain behavior of record.
